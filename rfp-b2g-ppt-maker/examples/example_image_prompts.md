# Example Image Prompts

```text
Create a single 16:9 PowerPoint slide image, 1920x1080.

Project:
서울시 지능형 CCTV 관제 고도화 사업

Presentation section:
Ⅱ. 기술 및 기능

Slide title:
목표 시스템 전체 아키텍처

Top breadcrumb:
Reserve a clean navy header area without rendering Korean text.

Main gradient title bar:
Reserve a thin red-orange-yellow accent bar without text.

Layout:
White canvas. Top navy header, left aligned title space, large central architecture diagram, bottom row of three small evidence chips. Keep 80px margins.

Main visual:
Flat architecture flow from CCTV video sources on the left to video ingestion, VLM analysis, event/risk decision, control center dashboard, and feedback data store on the right. Add a separate lower lane for monitoring operators and audit logs. Use circular nodes connected by thin arrows.

Quantitative metric card placeholders:
Three empty metric cards at bottom right, each with icon, number placeholder, and source-note placeholder.

Design style:
Korean public-sector B2G proposal deck, white background, navy header, restrained red-orange-yellow accents, thin gray dividers, dense but clean alignment.

Negative prompt:
No readable Korean text, no fake numbers, no futuristic city background, no 3D robots, no neon, no crowded labels.
```
